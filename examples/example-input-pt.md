## Slide 1 — Cover
LEVVA
Financial OS for freelancers
Founders: Bruno (CEO), Ana (CTO)

## Slide 2 — Problem
Freelancers in Brazil struggle with:
- Tax confusion (MEI vs PJ)
- Mixing personal and business money
- Late invoice payments
- Cashflow uncertainty

## Slide 3 — Solution
Levva is the operating system for freelancers.
- Auto-categorize income/expenses
- Generate invoices in 1 click
- Tax projection (MEI / PJ / Simples)
- Cashflow forecast via AI
